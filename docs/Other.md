# Other

Use Other for practical jobs and things to bring.

Items are grouped into categories. Each row shows its name, note, assignment and status as text. Use the pencil to open a row’s editor. Save keeps the changes; Cancel discards them. Typing does not save automatically.

Use a category’s + to open a new item above its list. Category arrows change the order, the pencil changes its name, and the Delete action at the lower right of its editor removes an empty category. Save/Cancel stay on the left.

The editor matches Food with name/category, notes/assignment and status fields, then Save and Cancel. Other names and notes currently use one shared text; no translation globes are added.

Delete removes an unused item after confirmation. Assigned items and work already in progress use Archive instead. Archiving preserves assignments and progress. Show archived records sits beside Expand all / Collapse all; enable it to find an archived item and Restore it. A category can be deleted only when it contains no items, including archived ones.

Review the assignments and statuses as your planning progresses.
