# Frequently asked questions

**Why can’t I edit?**

Your account may have View only access to this section, or its local editor may be closed. Ask a Full administrator if access needs changing.

**Why is Save disabled?**

An existing record normally needs a change first. Required fields must also be completed. A new prefilled reminder is already a draft that can be saved.

**Did opening WhatsApp or Messages send the invitation?**

No. Review and send it in that app, then return to record the sending.

**Why has a food item disappeared for guests?**

It may be fully assigned, hidden, unavailable at their venue, or outside their household’s food/session eligibility.

**Does a venue assignment invite a household to a session?**

No. Food availability and session invitations are separate choices.

**How do I get a recipe as PDF?**

Open Recipe, then Print in the read-only dialog. Use your device’s print/save options. Use the recipe’s pencil to edit; Preview prints the current draft.

**Where do I translate an item?**

Use the globe beside Name in the food item editor, or the Languages page. Shared type/unit wording is translated globally.

**Does a backup include account passwords?**

No. Account and hosting recovery need separate arrangements.

**Does restoring publish the event or re-enable all administrators?**

No. The event stays unpublished. Restored administrator references are disabled until explicitly reviewed and enabled.

**Does choosing “I can bring food” reserve anything?**

No. It saves willingness immediately. Eligible households can then select items if the detailed list is open; they review and confirm those separately.

**Why do confirmed replies remain visible?**

Attendance and food selections stay as read-only lists. Contact the organizer if you need a correction.

**Can someone see the event without an invitation?**

The guest entry page is public, but event details require a valid active invitation and a published event. Authorized administrator preview works while unpublished.
